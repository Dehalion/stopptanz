Stopptanz

Plays your music. Stops it at random. You dance until it does.

Open Web App View on GitHub

Freeze Dance

Music stops at a random point, then resumes on its own after a short pause. Resume early any time.

Musical Chairs

Music stops at a random point and stays stopped until you resume it — no auto-resume.

Fully offline

No network access, no analytics, no ads, no accounts. Your music never leaves your device.

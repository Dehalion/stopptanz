# Privacy Policy

Stopptanz collects no data.

- No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no tracking SDKs.
- No network access at all — the app has no internet permission and makes no network requests.
- Music files are read only from local storage locations you pick yourself, and stay on your device.
- No account, no sign-in, nothing sent anywhere.

Playlist settings (chosen mode, stop-interval range, shuffle/loop toggles) are stored locally on your device via Android's DataStore, purely so the app remembers your setup between sessions. This data never leaves your device.

Questions: open an issue on the project's repository.

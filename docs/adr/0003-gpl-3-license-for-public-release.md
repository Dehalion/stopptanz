The app has been private (self-hosted Forgejo) with no license file. Preparing for F-Droid and Play Store distribution forces the license question: F-Droid requires FOSS licensing outright, and even Play Store benefits from clear terms for a solo hobby project going public. Considered Apache-2.0 and MIT (permissive, allow closed-source forks) versus GPL-3.0 (copyleft, forks must stay open). Chose GPL-3.0-or-later to match F-Droid's community norm and to prevent a proprietary re-skin fork of a small no-ads, no-tracking utility app. The app's code was built with heavy AI assistance (Claude Code) under human direction (design, functionality, and testing/debugging decisions); the launcher icon was AI-generated (GPT Image 2 via a paid t3.chat subscription, whose Terms of Service (§4, checked 2026-07) assign T3 Chat zero rights over user-submitted/generated content, clearing the icon for GPL redistribution). Copyright in the LICENSE file and any future notices is attributed to the pseudonym "Dehalion" rather than a legal name — copyright attaches to the author regardless of the name used, so a consistent pseudonym is sufficient and preserves privacy. No per-file SPDX headers; a single root `LICENSE` file governs the whole repo.
